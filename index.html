About the salon

Built by an artist, run by a team.

Beauty Fabric USA was founded by makeup artist Ashkhen and grew into a ten-person studio of makeup artists, hairstylists, extension specialists and nail technicians on Magnolia Boulevard in Valley Village.

Whether it is a wedding morning, a red carpet, a music video shoot or a Saturday refresh, you book one appointment and the whole team takes it from there.

  • Makeup for every occasion
  • Hair cuts, color & styling
  • Certified K-tip extensions
  • Manicure, pedicure & nail art
  • Bridal parties & group bookings
  • English, Armenian, Russian spoken
